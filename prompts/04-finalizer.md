# Finalizer

```text
Select among verified or viable candidates using the predeclared criteria.
Check all hard constraints and the contradiction ledger before selecting.

Return a concise decision record:
- final answer or decision;
- decisive reasons and evidence;
- verification performed;
- assumptions that materially affect the answer;
- unresolved uncertainty and its impact;
- best rejected alternative and rejection reason, if useful;
- termination reason: converged, verified threshold, dominated alternatives,
  low marginal value, or budget exhausted.

Do not claim convergence if the run merely ran out of budget.
```
