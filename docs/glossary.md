# Glossary

- **Candidate** — a possible answer, plan, hypothesis, or partial solution.
- **Controller** — policy that selects the next operator or stops.
- **Dimension** — a task-relevant lens used to produce structured diversity.
- **Edge** — a typed and justified relation between nodes.
- **Frontier** — currently applicable operations awaiting selection.
- **Ledger** — structured record of constraints, assumptions, contradictions, evidence, and unknowns.
- **Node** — a compact reasoning artifact with provenance and status.
- **Operator** — a state transformation such as diverge, challenge, verify, or synthesize.
- **Path** — a dependency or derivation sequence through part of the web.
- **Synthesis** — a provenance-preserving combination of compatible partial solutions.
- **Thought** — an externally useful intermediate artifact, not necessarily a private token-by-token reasoning trace.
- **Verification reserve** — budget protected for checking and rendering the final result.
- **Web** — the sparse typed network plus its controller, ledgers, dimensions, and budgets.
