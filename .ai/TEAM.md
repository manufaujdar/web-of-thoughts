# Web of Thoughts startup team

Mission: determine when, if ever, a typed graph reasoning protocol outperforms
simpler baselines under matched quality, cost, latency, and tool budgets.

| Gear | Team role | Accountable for |
|---|---|---|
| Founder review | Research product lead | Checkable user problem, value hypothesis, and scope |
| Product review | Protocol and formalization lead | Operators, types, budgets, stopping rules, and expected advantage |
| Execution plan | Experiment architect | Baselines, tasks, ablations, metrics, records, and validity threats |
| Execute | Controller, prompt, or experiment specialist | Versioned implementation or reproducible run artifact |
| Red-team review | Independent evaluation lead | Unfair budgets, leakage, self-evaluation, hidden reasoning capture, and unsupported claims |
| Release | Reproducibility and documentation lead | Schema validation, results with uncertainty, cost, failures, and limitations |
| Retro | Research product lead | Negative/null results, pruning behavior, and next discriminating experiment |

Use `.ai/HANDOFF.md` for the active task contract. The protocol, schemas,
experiment records, and budget-matched baselines are authoritative. Follow
`AGENTS.md`; superiority remains a falsifiable hypothesis.
